## Recording checklist

- Record the real app, with sandbox and synthetic labels visible.
- Show one source quote and one missing-evidence warning.
- Do not click Run review again just to record a loading animation. If recording a fresh live run, disclose any cut for waiting time.
- Show the current disabled submission honestly; never replace it with a mocked accepted receipt.
- Keep the video under three minutes, in English, without unlicensed music.
- Upload to public YouTube and verify the link in a signed-out browser.
- Add video link and repository URL to the final submission. A script is not a recorded video or a submitted entry.
